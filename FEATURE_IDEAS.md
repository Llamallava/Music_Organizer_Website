# Feature Ideas

A running list of potential features for the app. Not prioritized — just a backlog to pull from.

---

## Discovery & Exploration

- **Similar albums / rabbit hole mode** — after finishing an album review, suggest similar albums (by genre, era, artist) to add next
- **Random album picker** — "I don't know what to listen to" button that picks a random saved album
- **Decade/era browser** — browse your saved albums filtered by release decade
- **Genre tagging on albums** — not just songs but whole albums can have genre tags

## Stats & Insights

- **Listening stats dashboard** — average score by artist, score distribution, most-reviewed genres, scoring trends over time
- **Top 10 / tier list builder** — drag-and-drop interface to rank your saved albums against each other
- **Score history** — track when you change a score so you can see how your opinion of an album evolved
- **Most tagged words** — word cloud or list of your most-used tags across all songs

## Social

- **Public profile page** — shareable link showing your reviews and scores (opt-in)
- **Compare with a friend** — side-by-side view of scores for albums you've both reviewed
- **Activity feed** — see when friends add or review albums

## Organization

- **Custom shelves / collections** — group albums into named collections beyond just playlists (e.g. "Desert Island Picks", "Guilty Pleasures")
- **Album status** — mark an album as "finished", "in progress", or "want to revisit"
- **Bulk tag editor** — apply or remove a tag across multiple songs at once

## Review Features

- **Review templates** — save a set of prompts/questions you always want to answer when reviewing an album, auto-populated into the notes field
- **Highlight quotes** — mark a specific lyric line as a favorite, separate from the full notes
- **Track ranking within an album** — after reviewing all tracks, drag to rank them 1 through N
- **Revisit reminders** — flag an album to come back to in X months, get a nudge on the dashboard

## Utility

- **Export** — download your reviews and scores as CSV or JSON for personal backup
- **Import from last.fm** — pull in scrobble history to pre-populate a "have listened to" list
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — navigate tracks, save sections, toggle interlude without touching the mouse
- **Offline support / PWA** — install as a home screen app, cache reviews for offline reading
